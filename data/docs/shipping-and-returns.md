# Shipping, Returns and Warranty

## Shipping times and costs
| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (US) | 3–5 business days | $6.95, free on orders over $75 |
| Express (US) | 1–2 business days | $18.00 |
| Canada | 6–10 business days | $14.00, free on orders over $150 |

Orders placed before 1pm Central Time on a business day ship the same day. We ship with UPS and FedEx. You receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

We do not currently ship outside the United States and Canada.

## Returns
You can return any unused item in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.
- Return shipping is free for US customers; we email you a prepaid label.
- Canadian customers pay return shipping.
- Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the return reaches our warehouse.
- Final sale items (marked "Final Sale" on the product page) cannot be returned.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement at no cost, or a full refund if the item is out of stock. You do not need to return damaged items.

## Warranty
All electrical products (Aurora Table Lamp, Mist Aroma Diffuser) have a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Textiles and tableware have a 1-year warranty. The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse or accidental damage.
