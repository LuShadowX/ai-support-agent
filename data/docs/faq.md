# Nimbus Home — Frequently Asked Questions

## About us
Nimbus Home is an online store for calm, well-made home goods: lighting, textiles, tableware and aroma products. We are based in Austin, Texas and ship across the United States and Canada.

## Payments
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. All prices are in US dollars. We do not accept cash on delivery or cryptocurrency.

## Discounts
New subscribers to our newsletter get 10% off their first order. Discounts cannot be combined and cannot be applied to orders that have already been placed.

## Changing or cancelling an order
You can change or cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it by contacting support. After 2 hours the order is sent to our warehouse and can no longer be changed; you can return it instead once it arrives.

## Gift wrapping
Gift wrapping is available for $5 per order at checkout. We include a handwritten note on request and never put prices in gift packages.

## Care instructions
- Cloudknit Throw Blanket: machine wash cold, gentle cycle, lay flat to dry.
- Linen Cushion Cover: machine wash at 40°C, low tumble dry. Linen softens with every wash.
- Stoneware Mug Set: dishwasher and microwave safe.
- Aurora Table Lamp: wipe with a dry cloth only.
